2. MODIFIED FILES (17 files)
================================================================================

[MODIFIED] app/services/prompts.py
  - Added centralized prompt builders `format_language_directive()`,
    `build_conversational_system_prompt()`, and `build_rag_system_prompt()` so
    the assistant's identity, tone, dynamic language adaptation, conversation
    memory context, and factual grounding rules are controlled centrally rather
    than through scattered hardcoded strings.

[MODIFIED] app/providers/llm.py
  - Fixed `OpenAICompatibleLlmProvider.generate_conversational_reply()` so it
    calls the configured LLM (`_post_completions`) with centralized system
    instructions (`build_conversational_system_prompt`) and bounded conversation
    history instead of delegating to `LocalLlmProvider`.
  - Updated `OpenAICompatibleLlmProvider.generate_answer()` to use
    `build_rag_system_prompt()`.
  - Overhauled `LocalLlmProvider.generate_conversational_reply()` and
    `LocalLlmProvider.generate_answer()` to remove robotic prefixes
    (`"Hamare verified details ke mutabik:"`, `"हमारे सत्यापित रिकॉर्ड के अनुसार:"`),
    handle `MEMORY_RECALL`, `TRANSLATE_PREVIOUS`, `LANGUAGE_SWITCH`, `IDENTITY`,
    `CAPABILITIES`, `LANGUAGE_ABILITY`, and `CHITCHAT`, and support ordinal plan
    resolution (`first plan`, `second plan`) and plan comparisons.

[MODIFIED] app/services/conversations.py
  - Upgraded `detect_language(text, prior_language)` and added
    `extract_requested_language(text)` to support dynamic language switching
    without a fixed language allowlist.
  - Added `classify_conversational_sub_intent()` (`IDENTITY`, `CAPABILITIES`,
    `LANGUAGE_ABILITY`, `MEMORY_RECALL`, `LANGUAGE_SWITCH`, `TRANSLATE_PREVIOUS`,
    `CHITCHAT`) so conversational messages are never misrouted into RAG fallback.
  - Updated `update_memory_state()` to persist explicit language switches and
    preserve active business topics/entities across conversational turns.

[MODIFIED] app/services/chat.py
  - Updated `process_chat()` and `stream_validated_chat()` to propagate
    `preferred_language`, conversation summary, and tenant identity metadata,
    and route conversational and non-English out-of-scope turns through
    `self.llm.generate_conversational_reply()`.

[MODIFIED] app/services/grounding.py
  - Updated `sanitize_customer_facing_text()` to strip robotic prefixes
    (`"Hamare verified details ke mutabik:"`, `"हमारे सत्यापित रिकॉर्ड के अनुसार:"`,
    `"ہمارے تصدیق شدہ ریکارڈ کے مطابق:"`) as well as internal status labels.
  - Upgraded `GroundingService.validate_answer()` so natural multilingual and

[MODIFIED] app/services/advanced_rag.py
